When I first saw the Duo, I was not sold. Folding phones are nothing new, and it felt like another "Apple is trying to catch up" product.

Naturally, [my friend Josh](https://www.thenewsprint.co) got his hands on one immediately so I had the opportunity to play around with it. This began to soften my view toward a folding device.

Apple has done a phenomenal job of designing this device. It looks gorgeous, and the hinge appears to be an engineering marvel.
